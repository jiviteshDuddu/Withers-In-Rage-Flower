# Withers-In-Rage-Flower
A psychological countdown visual novel game in Renpy where the flower rage has 10 sec to live and in those 10 seconds, 1/2 of the 10 second which 5 seconds 5 different emotion flowers will come and ask to blend into me or remain as you and after few sec, it should what happened to you as you remained as rage flower or merged into a emotion flower
